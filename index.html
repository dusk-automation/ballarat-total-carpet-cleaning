How a Total clean works

Four steps, one visit.

  1. 01

    Pre-vacuum

    Grit, crumbs and whatever the kids dropped come up first, so the steam goes into the carpet, not the mess sitting on top. From $50.

  2. 02

    Steam clean

    Luke works through each room in steady, overlapping passes of the wand. You can watch the clean lines appear as he goes.

  3. 03

    Free deodorising

    Deodorising comes free with every carpet clean. In one customer’s words, “the whole place smelled clean.”

  4. 04

    Back to normal

    Furniture back where it lives and a room that feels new underfoot. The line that comes up again and again in his reviews: like new again.
