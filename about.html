About

Local, family run, and fussy about clean.

Ballarat Total Carpet Cleaning is Luke’s business, with his wife Loida beside him on the job.

Over 15 years of carpets.

Luke has been cleaning carpets for over 15 years and is fully trained and insured. These days he runs Ballarat Total Carpet Cleaning with his wife Loida, and customers often mention the two of them by name.

Most days are carpets, couches and rugs, with mattresses, NDIS cleaning and full vacate cleans when a rental changes hands. Prices are posted openly, quotes are free, and most jobs start with a phone call to Luke.

What customers keep coming back to: he turns up on time, he’s happy to have a chat, the price is fair, and the carpets look and smell like new.
