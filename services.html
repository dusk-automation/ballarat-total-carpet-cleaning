Questions

Good to know.

Can I get a price over the phone?

Yes. Call Luke on 0423 585 110 for an immediate quote over the phone.

Is deodorising extra?

No. Deodorising is included free with carpet cleaning.

What does an end of lease clean involve?

The whole property, not just the carpets. Asked about carpet shampoo, windows and the oven, Luke said: “We can do it all. Everything gets a wash down.” Prices start from $500.

Do I need to vacuum first?

You can leave it to Luke. A pre-vacuum is available from $50, so the steam clean goes into the carpet rather than the loose dirt on top.

Are you insured?

Yes. Ballarat Total Carpet Cleaning is fully trained and insured.

Which areas do you cover?

Ballarat. If you are just outside town, call and ask.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. Closed on Sundays.
